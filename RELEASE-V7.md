# Selection menus and Revenant healing

Publishes the playtested character carousel and compact stage-name carousel, with numbered level labels, for solo and multiplayer. Action descriptions remain visible beside the hunter and use Basic Attack terminology.

Fixes Blood Pact wasting its allowance at full health and the multiplayer actor-copy overwriting its healed-total counter. Adds floating +HP feedback and active healed-total HUD. Healing is bounded by missing player HP, actual damage dealt, the four-second duration and the existing 12 HP allowance.

Stage order and host-only multiplayer selection are preserved. Validation: carousel interactions, host/guest restrictions, class rendering, healing edge cases and complete two/four-player encounters on all maps.
